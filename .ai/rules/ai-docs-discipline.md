# Rule: ai-docs-discipline

> Planet Express Universal AI Rule (Core) — applies across Open Code, Antigravity, Claude, Codex, and Cursor.

- **One truth per concern**: Update canonical docs in place; NEVER spawn throwaway `NOTES.md`, `SUMMARY.md`, or duplicate design dumps.
- **Keep rules concise (<60 lines)**: Imperative DO/DON'T bullets only.
- **Context-selective rules**: Keep only active-stack rules in `.ai/rules/`; store reusable templates in `templates/rules/`.
